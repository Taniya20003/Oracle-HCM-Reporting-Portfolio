# Talent Management Analytics – Business Requirement

## 1. Business Background

Talent management activities require HR and People & Culture teams to review information from multiple sources to prepare talent-related reporting and tracking.

The existing process involved manual preparation and consolidation of information, requiring users to gather and validate data before producing the required talent management views. This created additional effort and made it more difficult to provide a consistent, self-service reporting experience.

The proposed Talent Management Analytics solution aims to provide a consolidated reporting view of relevant talent information and reduce the dependency on manual data preparation.

## 2. Business Problem

The existing talent reporting process required HR and People & Culture teams to manually consolidate information from multiple sources to prepare detailed and summary talent views.

This manual approach resulted in:

- Additional effort for data preparation and consolidation.
- Time spent validating and maintaining reporting information.
- Dependency on manually prepared trackers.
- Limited availability of a centralized, self-service reporting view.

A reporting solution was therefore required to provide users with a more consistent and consolidated way to access talent-related information.

## 3. Business Objective

The objective of the Talent Management Analytics solution is to provide HR and People & Culture teams with a centralized, self-service reporting solution for accessing relevant talent information.

The solution aims to:

- Reduce manual effort involved in preparing talent reports.
- Consolidate relevant talent information into a single reporting view.
- Provide consistent and easily accessible information for talent review activities.
- Enable users to analyze talent information using report filters and different reporting views.
- Support efficient preparation and review of talent management information.

## 4. Stakeholders

The Talent Management Analytics solution is intended to support users involved in talent management and review activities.

### Primary Stakeholders

- **HR Business Partners** – Use talent information to support workforce and talent-related discussions.
- **People & Culture / HR Teams** – Use the reporting solution to review and analyze talent information.
- **Talent Management Teams** – Use consolidated talent information to support talent review activities.

### Reporting Users

Users should be able to access the relevant reporting views and apply available filters to analyze the information required for their business activities.

## 5. High-Level Requirements

The Talent Management Analytics solution should provide the following capabilities:

### 5.1 Employee Information

The report should provide relevant employee information required for talent analysis, such as:

- Employee identification details
- Employee name
- Employment-related information
- Manager information

### 5.2 Organizational Information

The report should provide organizational information to support analysis across the organization, including:

- Business Unit
- Department
- Legal Employer
- Organizational hierarchy

### 5.3 Talent Information

The solution should provide relevant talent information to support talent review activities, including:

- Talent ratings
- Talent-related classifications
- Historical talent information where applicable
- Relevant talent review information

### 5.4 Review Information

The report should provide information required to support talent review activities, including relevant review status and review-related attributes.

### 5.5 Filtering and Analysis

Users should be able to filter and analyze the report based on relevant business criteria, allowing them to focus on the population or information required for their analysis.

### 5.6 Reporting Views

The solution should support both detailed and summary reporting views to accommodate different reporting and analysis requirements.

### 5.7 Export

The reporting solution should support commonly required report output formats, such as Excel and PDF, where applicable.

## 6. Success Criteria

The Talent Management Analytics solution will be considered successful when:

- The report provides a consolidated view of the required talent management information.
- The information presented in the report is consistent with the relevant source data.
- Users can access the required information through a self-service reporting solution.
- Users can apply the available reporting filters to analyze the required population.
- The solution supports the required detailed and summary reporting views.
- The report can be exported in the required output formats.
- The report provides acceptable performance for normal business usage.
- The solution reduces the manual effort required to prepare talent management reporting.
