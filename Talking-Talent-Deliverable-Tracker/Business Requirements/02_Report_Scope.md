# Talent Management Analytics – Report Scope

## 1. Overview

This document defines the scope of the Talent Management Analytics reporting solution. It identifies the reporting capabilities, information areas, and high-level functionality included in the portfolio project.

The solution is designed to provide consolidated talent management reporting through detailed and summary reporting views.

## 2. In Scope

The following areas are included in the reporting solution:

### 2.1 Employee Information

- Employee identification details
- Employee name
- Employment-related information
- Manager information

### 2.2 Organizational Information

- Business Unit
- Department
- Legal Employer
- Organizational hierarchy

### 2.3 Talent Information

- Talent ratings
- Talent-related classifications
- Talent review information
- Historical talent information where applicable

### 2.4 Reporting Views

The solution includes:

- Detailed talent reporting
- Summary talent reporting
- Filter-based analysis

### 2.5 Report Outputs

The solution may support:

- Interactive reporting
- Excel output
- PDF output

## 3. Out of Scope

The following areas are outside the scope of this reporting project:

- Payroll and compensation processing.
- Benefits administration.
- Employee self-service transactions.
- Changes to employee or talent data.
- Talent management transactions or approvals.
- Creation or modification of talent profiles through the report.
- Real-time data updates from the report interface.

## 4. Assumptions

The following assumptions apply to the Talent Management Analytics reporting solution:

- The required talent and employee information is available in the relevant Oracle HCM source data.
- Users have the appropriate access to view the information available in the report.
- Reported information follows the applicable data security and access rules.
- The reporting solution uses available Oracle HCM data and reporting capabilities.
- Sample data used in this portfolio project is fictional and created for demonstration purposes.

## 5. Scope Summary

The Talent Management Analytics project focuses on providing a centralized reporting solution for talent-related information.

The scope includes detailed and summary reporting views, employee and organizational information, relevant talent information, filtering capabilities, and report output options.

The project does not include talent transactions or changes to source data. Its primary purpose is to demonstrate an enterprise reporting and analytics solution using Oracle HCM and Oracle BI Publisher.
