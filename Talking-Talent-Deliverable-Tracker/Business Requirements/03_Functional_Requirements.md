# Talent Management Analytics – Functional Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the functional requirements for the Talent Management Analytics reporting solution.

The requirements describe the expected reporting views, information categories, user filters, and reporting capabilities required to support talent management analysis.

The solution is designed to provide both detailed and summary reporting views based on the requirements defined for the Talent Management Analytics project.

## 2. Report Views

The Talent Management Analytics solution should provide multiple reporting views to support different levels of analysis.

### 2.1 Detailed Report

The detailed report should provide employee-level talent information.

The report should allow users to review relevant employee, organizational, and talent-related information in a structured format.

The detailed view should support analysis of individual employees and provide the necessary information required for talent management discussions.

### 2.2 Summary Report

The summary report should provide a consolidated view of the talent population.

The report should present summarized information to help users understand talent distribution and support higher-level analysis.

The summary view should complement the detailed report by providing a broader overview of the available talent information.

## 3. Data Requirements

The Talent Management Analytics solution should provide relevant information required to support detailed and summary talent analysis.

### 3.1 Employee Information

The detailed report should provide relevant employee information, including:

- Employee Number
- Employee Name
- Employment-related information
- Manager information

### 3.2 Organizational Information

The report should provide organizational information to support analysis across different areas of the organization, including:

- Business Unit
- Department
- Legal Employer
- Organizational hierarchy

### 3.3 Talent Information

The report should provide relevant talent-related information required for talent review activities, including:

- Talent Rating
- Previous Talent Rating
- Talent Evolution
- Talent-related classifications
- Talent review information

### 3.4 Additional Analysis Information

Where applicable, the report may provide additional information to support talent analysis, such as:

- Job information
- Grade
- Career-related information
- Performance-related information
- Review status

## 4. Filters and Prompts

The Talent Management Analytics solution should provide prompts and filters to help users analyze the relevant employee population.

### 4.1 Organizational Filters

Users should be able to filter the report based on relevant organizational criteria, such as:

- Business Unit
- Department
- Legal Employer

### 4.2 Talent and Review Filters

Users should be able to filter the report based on relevant talent management criteria, such as:

- Talent Review Year
- Talent Rating
- Review Status

### 4.3 Organizational Hierarchy Filters

Where applicable, users should be able to filter the report based on organizational hierarchy levels.

The available filters should allow users to focus the report output on the employee population relevant to their analysis.

## 5. User Interaction and Reporting Behaviour

The Talent Management Analytics solution should provide a user-friendly reporting experience that allows users to review and analyze talent information efficiently.

Users should be able to:

- Apply available prompts and filters before or during report analysis.
- Review detailed employee-level talent information.
- Review summarized talent information.
- Navigate between the available reporting views where applicable.
- Use the report to analyze the employee population relevant to their business needs.

The report should present information in a structured and readable format to support talent review and analysis activities.

## 6. Report Output and Export Requirements

The Talent Management Analytics solution should support reporting outputs that allow users to review and share talent information.

The reporting solution should provide:

- Interactive report viewing.
- Export capability for detailed reporting information.
- Excel output for further analysis, where required.
- PDF output for review and sharing, where applicable.

The report output should maintain a structured and readable format across the supported output types.

## 7. Functional Requirement Summary

The Talent Management Analytics solution should provide a consolidated reporting experience for talent management activities.

The solution includes:

- Detailed and summary reporting views.
- Employee, organizational, and talent-related information.
- Relevant filters and prompts.
- User-friendly reporting and analysis capabilities.
- Report output and export functionality.

These functional requirements will provide the foundation for the solution design, data model, SQL development, and BI Publisher report design.
